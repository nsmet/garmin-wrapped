    About your Garmin recap

    What is Garmin Wrapped?

    A colourful year in review made from your Garmin Connect activities. An independent MINI WATT project. Save your favourite stories as 1080 × 1920 PNG cards.

    Can I see my Garmin year in review before December?

    Yes. Your recap covers the current year so far, in your device’s time zone. Only highlights supported by your recorded data appear.

    Is everything processed locally?

    Your recap is built and kept in this tab’s memory. The Garmin import passes securely through this site’s server; your key and activity data are not saved on our servers. Refreshing or closing the tab clears your recap. Downloaded cards stay on your device.

    Garmin Connect · Personal API

    Your key.
    Your start line.

    Get your Personal API key on the Garmin Connect website. Use your own account’s key, not your Garmin password.

    Open Garmin’s key page (opens in a new tab)
    1. 01

      Sign in to Garmin Connect

      The link opens Settings → Personal API Key in your browser. Sign in if prompted.

    2. 02

      Create your key

      Select Create Key, review Garmin’s terms and accept if you agree. Choose an expiry (30, 90 or 180 days, or one year), then select Create.

    3. 03

      Copy it before you leave

      Garmin shows the key only once. Copy it and keep it somewhere secure, then select Done. The field here is for that Personal API key.

    Lost, expired or deleted your key?

    Return to Garmin’s key page to create a replacement. If you lost an existing key, delete it first. Deleting a key also disables its access tokens.

    Data & privacy

    Your data.
    Your call.

    Garmin Wraped is an independent MINI WATT project. Each visitor connects only their own Garmin account using their own Personal API key. Garmin Wraped is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin.

    Your key

    Your key is exchanged with Garmin by this site’s server over HTTPS. The server retains no activity or health records. A secure, encrypted connection cookie expires after 20 minutes and is cleared when the import finishes or you disconnect. Visitors have separate connection sessions. Tabs in the same browser share a connection cookie; import one account at a time.

    Your recap

    The recap lives only in this tab’s memory. Refreshing or closing the tab removes it. “Clear my data” removes the recap, export preview, and connection cookie. Downloaded PNG files and shares you send remain on your device or in the receiving app.

    Health cards

    Daily steps, floors climbed, sleep, heart rate and overnight HRV are included automatically when available. Sharing any health card requires a separate confirmation for that card. Exports contain only the shown metric and approved copy: no names, Garmin identifiers, source references, or precise locations.

    Coverage

    Activity dates use your automatically detected device time zone. Daily wellness, sleep and HRV use Garmin’s recorded calendar days. Missing measurements are omitted. A complete API import means all pages returned for the requested date window; it cannot establish that Garmin holds every activity you performed.

    Application error logs contain request IDs, route names, error codes, and timings only. Cloudflare hosts this app and processes connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver it and limit requests. No advertising or analytics SDK is installed. Fonts are served directly by this app; no third-party font requests are made. Disconnecting does not revoke your Garmin key. You can revoke it on Garmin’s key page.
