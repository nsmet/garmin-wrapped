# mini yearly recap — Softform and One Line

Eight still concept screens: cycling, sleep, nutrition and a portrait summary for each direction. These are art-direction proposals; the motion described here has not been implemented. All metrics are fictional, shared between both directions so the comparison is about design.

| Chapter | Main metric | Supporting context | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cycling | 8,642 km cycled | 126 rides; 412 hours | You went the distance. |
| Sleep | 7h 24m average recorded sleep | 326 recorded nights | You made time to recharge. |

Cycling stays the lead chapter and the largest achievement on the summary. Sleep and nourishment extend the athlete's story. Copy reports records without claiming that sleep or eating caused a performance outcome. The nutrition scene appears only when relevant records exist; another available chapter can replace it. Missing records are never converted to zeros. Sleep averages use recorded nights, not every calendar day.

## Softform

Use saturated vermilion, cornflower blue and buttery yellow sculptural forms against quiet lilac and peach backgrounds. Materials are tactile matte silicone, with rounded edges and restrained deformation. Use athletic sans typography, strong metric hierarchy and one focal sculpture. Every object should retain its identity as it transforms.

1. Cycling: a tilted vermilion wheel with a blue hub and low yellow ramp. Entry follows the wheel rolling into place; it settles as the distance becomes readable. Keep the final stat static while the user reads.
2. Cycling to sleep: slow the wheel, turn its opening toward the camera, compress the rim and unfold it into low rounded bands. Change the light gradually with the transformation. Approximately 1.2–1.6 seconds of continuous movement; no cut to a new unrelated object.
3. Sleep: the stacked forms settle gently. Avoid perpetual breathing loops that would compete with the reading pause. The scene concerns recorded sleep, not a diagnosis or composite recovery score.
4. Sleep to nutrition: the bands separate slightly, rotate and fit into a low plate-like assembly. Approximately 1.0–1.4 seconds. The pieces are a visual metaphor, not measured macro proportions.
5. Nutrition to summary: the pieces lift and assemble into one compact emblem. Pull the camera back as the portrait typography and selected stats enter. Approximately 1.4–1.8 seconds. End completely still, ready to export.

Softform share artwork is a compact arch/wheel, blue core and yellow base. A consistent silhouette makes the campaign recognizable even when personal colors or arrangements vary.

## One Line

One broad matte ribbon, cobalt on its outside and coral on the reverse, remains visible across every chapter. Saturated yellow introduces the sporting chapter and returns for the summary; sleep shifts toward lavender and nutrition toward apricot. All changes preserve the ribbon's physical continuity.

1. Cycling: a forward-tilted wheel/track loop. The camera follows its leading edge into the composition, then stops for the distance reveal.
2. Cycling to sleep: maintain the camera's view of the same edge as the loop opens and lowers into a long S-shaped fold. Approximately 1.4–1.8 seconds. Large arcs and controlled easing keep the transformation legible.
3. Sleep: two broad horizontal spans suggest the duration of rest. A quantitative sleep chart, if added later, needs explicitly labeled lengths and a scale; this sculpture is not such a chart.
4. Sleep to nutrition: sweep around the fold as it curls upward into a simple open vessel. Approximately 1.2–1.6 seconds. Keep one visible free end so its identity as the same strip is obvious.
5. Nutrition to summary: lift the vessel and unwind it into the compact final S gesture. Ease the camera into the portrait crop and stop. Approximately 1.5–2 seconds.

One Line share artwork uses a single large ribbon gesture with clear negative space. The bright background and strong silhouette should remain recognizable in a small social preview.

## Screen composition

Desktop chapter studies use a landscape composition: branding and chapter indicator at the top, copy and metric at the left, one object at the right, navigation at the bottom. Keep the main metric visible without waiting for an animation. The supporting statistic line is secondary; it never becomes a separate grid of cards.

On mobile, recompose rather than shrink: chapter label and heading first, a bounded object area in the middle, main metric with supporting context below, then bottom navigation. Reduce sculpture depth and lateral camera travel to avoid cropping the object or inducing excessive motion on a narrow screen. Maintain readable labels and touch targets. Share the same narrative and art direction across layouts.

Allow forward and backward navigation, keyboard access and an explicit reduced-motion composition. Reduced motion uses stable keyframes with short transitions. Do not hijack normal page scrolling or require sound. Animation should pause when the page is not visible. A lower-detail render retains the same composition and typography.

## Summary and sharing

The two summary images are 9:16 portrait concepts and should double as the visual composition for the final mobile scene. The export artwork contains no buttons or browser chrome. The website places Choose stats, Save image and Share controls outside the export area. Selection is local until the user shares; sharing is not automatic.

Offer a 1080 × 1920 story export and a separately composed 1080 × 1350 feed export. Keep the logo and all selected metrics within generous safe margins. At small sizes, the year, cycling achievement and artwork should read before the secondary health metrics.

The sample summary includes sleep and nutrition to demonstrate the full concept. Let the rider choose what appears; a sports-only export could substitute elevation or active days when available. Do not show exact route locations on the default share artwork. Use a quiet mini signature and an actual product link once the public address exists. Avoid an invented URL or a non-functional QR code in mockups.

An optional short animated share loop can be explored after the still export works. Keep essential numbers stable and visible long enough to read.

## Decision

Softform emphasizes friendly physicality and clearer object-based chapter metaphors. One Line offers the clearest continuity for a seamless cinematic journey. Evaluate the same three-scene transition prototype on a phone before choosing a final direction; stills alone cannot establish animation quality or performance.
